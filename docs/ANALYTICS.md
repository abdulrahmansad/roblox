# Analytics foundation

No external analytics service is wired in this phase. Future gameplay should emit internal, server-owned domain events through a narrow analytics boundary so a provider can be added later without coupling gameplay to it. Avoid collecting personally identifying data; define event schemas and retention with the selected provider before integration.

Candidate events: first join, tutorial completion, first hatch, first mutation, first upgrade, first Raid participation, first Raid completion, successful theft deposit, creature stolen from a player, successful Tag, session duration, return session, purchase funnel, purchase completion, and progression milestones. Record mutation tier and whether Rare-or-better pity was active so observed Mythic frequency can be compared with its exact 0.1% per-roll chance; do not track a Mythic pity counter because none exists. Track catalyst grants with their server-validated source and enforce that only a successful theft deposit grants one, at most once per thief per Raid; a Tag has no catalyst reward. For Welcome protection, record server-derived protection status at a theft attempt, participation start reason (`raid_start` or `arrival_grace_expired`), the Raid ID, completion boundary, and whether protection expired while connected or was finalized after a later profile load. Record arrival grace application and expiry, including whether it expired before RAID-to-RESOLUTION. Do not collect precise position or use client-reported eligibility. Record event context needed to measure cohorts and outcomes, not raw secrets or arbitrary client payloads. Client events can inform presentation but cannot prove rewards or purchases.
