# Analytics foundation

No external analytics service is wired in this phase. Future gameplay should emit internal, server-owned domain events through a narrow analytics boundary so a provider can be added later without coupling gameplay to it. Avoid collecting personally identifying data; define event schemas and retention with the selected provider before integration.

Candidate events: first join, tutorial completion, first hatch, first mutation, first upgrade, first Raid completion, successful theft deposit, creature stolen from a player, successful Tag, session duration, return session, purchase funnel, purchase completion, and progression milestones. Record the mutation tier and whether Rare-or-better pity was active so observed Mythic frequency can be compared with its exact 0.1% per-roll chance; do not track a Mythic pity counter because none exists. Track catalyst grants with their server-validated source and enforce that only a successful theft deposit grants one, at most once per thief per Raid; a Tag has no catalyst reward. Record whether Welcome protection was active at a theft attempt and when the first completed Raid ends it, plus when arrival grace is applied/expired. Do not collect precise position or use client-reported eligibility. Record event context needed to measure cohorts and outcomes, not raw secrets or arbitrary client payloads. Client events can inform presentation but cannot prove rewards or purchases.
