# Economy foundation

The final economy is intentionally undecided. Do not add balances, costs, reward schedules, or progression formulas during foundation work. Future gameplay values belong in centralized server-owned configuration and must be validated by the server.

Before tuning, measure currency generation per minute, creature income, mutation and upgrade costs, time to first reward and first upgrade, progression curves, currency inflation, sinks, and the effect of paid acceleration. Compare free and paid progression to ensure free players can enjoy and progress through the complete core loop without coercion.
