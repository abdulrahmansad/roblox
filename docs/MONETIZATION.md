# Monetization foundation

No products, prices, IDs, or purchase flows are created in this phase. Future revenue may include eligible Roblox engagement-based Creator Rewards and optional Robux purchases.

Free players must be able to enjoy the complete core gameplay and progress meaningfully without being punished into paying. Paid options may offer cosmetics, convenience, temporary acceleration, optional capacity, prestige, or fun extras. Avoid paywalls on recovery from unfairly stolen progression, impossible free progression, unbeatable purchased endgame state, deceptive offers, and accidental repeat purchases.

Keep product catalog/configuration and receipt handling server-side. Developer Product fulfillment must be idempotent and receipt-verified; Game Pass entitlements must be checked by the server. Separate purchase presentation from fulfillment, and cover retries, duplicate receipts, and errors with tests before enabling products.
